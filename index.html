About StandardsBridge

Franchisor-Controlled Operational Intelligence

StandardsBridge provides a franchisor-controlled intelligence layer that continuously analyses operational data across multiple locations using predictive AI.

Legacy Franchise Monitoring

Traditional franchise monitoring relies heavily on:

  • ✕ Manual audits: Periodic site visits that capture only isolated moments in time.
  • ✕ Spreadsheet reporting: Stale, labor-intensive data collated days after week-ends.
  • ✕ Periodic compliance checks: Infrequent reviews leaving weeks of operational blindness.
  • ✕ Site-level systems: Controlled by individual locations, hindering estate-wide governance.
